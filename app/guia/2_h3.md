# 2 · What MiniMax H3 does (what matters to you)

> Measured in our own tests, 19–27 Oct 2026, on an RTX 3060 12 GB and RunPod 24 GB GPUs. ✅ = controlled test · 🟢 = seen
> several times. If something here disagrees with what the app does today, the app wins.

## The division of work

- **The code decides WHEN** (the times of every cut and word, written into the prompt). **H3 decides WHAT is seen.** Without
  times in the prompt, cuts come out at random; with them, H3 cuts within ~0.1–0.45 s of the plan ✅.
- **The written description weighs more than the reference image** ✅. Same prompt without references gave the same look; a
  reference with a minimal prompt gave one plain shot with no effect. A reference only works when the text asks for what it
  shows.
- H3 copies the song but **does not dance to the beat**: it moves at its own pace. It does cut on the strongest hit (the drop)
  even if the plan says otherwise 🟢 — put a cut there.

## Length and frame rate

- **24 fps, and only 17·k+5 frames**: 22 (0.917 s), 39, 56, 73, 90, 107, **124 (5.167 s)**, … **243 (10.125 s)**. The app
  snaps any length to the nearest valid one.
- **~5 s per clip is where H3 follows the plan.** At 8 s with many references it compresses the timeline and skips shots ✅;
  in a 12 s pod render one shot disappeared and the last one started early.
- **About 10 shots per 10 s.** With 20 shots in 9 s, H3 made ~10 ✅. Shots of 0.4–0.5 s get lost 🟢. Each shot ≥ 0.4 s.
- Two consecutive shots asking for the same thing (same framing, same camera, no new gesture) become one shot: H3 does not cut 🟢.

## Format, resolution and memory

- **Any aspect ratio** (vertical, horizontal or square): the creative picks it in «Resolución» (⇅ swaps width and height). 1 MP vertical = **768×1344**, horizontal = **1344×768**; quick tests: **544×960** / **960×544** (0.52 MP). Sizes are multiples of 32.
- **1 MP keeps identity better and with more detail than 480p** 🟢 (in a 10 s reel edit, at 480p another face slipped into
  two shots; at 1 MP it did not).
- What fits (renders measured):

| GPU | Fits | Does not fit |
|---|---|---|
| RTX 3060 12 GB (this PC) | 22 f at 0.52 MP (~2–3 min) · 8 s at 0.4 MP (~9 min) · 5 s at 1 MP (~13–18 min) | 8 s at 0.8–1 MP |
| RTX 3090 24 GB (RunPod) | 12 s at 1 MP | 12 s at 1.2 MP (OOM) |

- **The local LLM (:8080) and ComfyUI share the 12 GB.** While the LLM works, the app's queue waits. Check estado first.

## References (RefMods)

- A reference is a RefMod card in the Stack. H3 receives at most **9 pictures and 3 videos**. Tags follow the order of the
  Stack, one counter for pictures and one for videos: `<Picture 1>`, `<Video 1>`…
- **Few and clean references per clip.** References with text, logos or recognisable faces get copied (H3 copies concrete
  things: logos, names, objects) ✅. A typography reference with its word inside reproduced that word ✅ (WRAPSTARZ). Erase
  text from a reference rather than blurring it: H3 reads blurred text too.
- **Lowering a RefMod's strength does not weaken it, it blurs it.** Control framing by cropping the image, not by strength.
- A **«cajita» (box)** is several photos in one RefMod, received as `<Video N>`; good for a character's identity ✅. It is
  **not a video**: no motion. An effect that moves needs a real clip 🟢.
- **A whole edit as a reference** 🟢✅: compressed (RefMod clip in «Compressed» mode, ~6,000 tokens), H3 copies its style and
  follows your shots; uncompressed, it copies pieces as they are. Works with long reels with many scenes; a short reel of few
  worlds makes H3 repeat that reel in its order.
- **Effects that change the drawing work** (silhouette, manga, halftone, ink, line art) 🟢. Editing effects (glitch, zoom, VHS,
  flashes) barely appear, or only at the start; they need an example clip. To copy an effect, describe its **cause**, not its
  result («a flat fill that switches colour on every beat») 🟢.
- **The character photo's background bleeds in** 🟢: on white-background photos, plain studio backgrounds work; scenic
  backgrounds appear only in the first frame. **A clean shop photo of a product comes out faithful**; a crop from another image
  does not 🟢.

## The seed

- **The seed weighs a lot** 🟢✅. The same recipe came out right with one seed and wrong with another (the animated title, 26
  Oct). **Changing the resolution also changes the draw**, even with the same seed number. Rule: after one failure, try another
  seed (or queue 2–3 seeds with generar(n=3), if the creative agrees) before touching the prompt.

## What fails (and what to do instead)

- **Negations** («no zoom», «never covering») can cause exactly that 🟢 → describe what does happen.
- **A short camera after a moving one** («holds still») lets H3 finish the previous shot's movement in this one ✅ → describe
  the still camera in detail: «stays locked off on a tripod at the same distance for the whole shot, keeping her entire body
  framed from head to sandals until the last frame».
- **Framing beats camera** ✅: «extreme close-up» + a camera that says «full portrait» → the framing wins. Ask both the same.
- **Invented athletic actions** (jumps, spins, arms up, fast hands) → deformed bodies and hands. Poses held still with the
  camera moving work far better (Vera recipe ✅).
- **Ambiguous words become things**: «wet red strands stuck to her cheeks» → red liquid on the face (twice) ✅; «against a
  rain-slicked asphalt background» + «grounded posture» → the character lying on the floor ✅.
- **«Photorealistic»** in a style line → realistic backgrounds where illustration was wanted.
- **Asking H3 for montage grammar** (flash cuts, snap zoom, shake, speed ramps) → it does not do it.
- **Asking for whooshes and impact hits in the soundscape** put bangs into the song → keep «Sonido» empty (just the music) and
  the video's audio on «Tu canción».
- **Typos and wrong pronouns** («his arm» for a girl, «wihite») are read literally 🟢.
- **Far shots lose the character**: close shots read best; full-body pose references pull shots to medium distance.
