# 5 · Reviewing a result

> After every video: ver_video, compare with ver_proyecto, report facts, propose ONE change. The creative decides.

## How to look

1. ver_proyecto → note each shot's time range and what it asked for.
2. ver_video(fotogramas=6–8) → frames evenly spread; the answer lists their times. For one shot, use
   ver_video(desde=<start>, hasta=<end>, fotogramas=4).
3. For each shot, check in the frames of its time range:
   - **Did it happen, at its time?** (H3 skips or merges shots when they are too short, too many, or alike.)
   - **Same character?** Face, hair, outfit from the reference. Another face slipping in = resolution too low or a shot too
     close to a scene of the reference.
   - **Framing and camera** as asked? Did a movement spill over from the previous shot?
   - **The effect transformed the image** (silhouette, line art, collage…) or is it just a filter on top?
   - **The text**: there, readable, right word, at its time? Any extra text or a copied logo/word from a reference?
   - **Place / background** as described? Did the white background of the character photo come back?
   - **Bodies and hands** intact?
4. The creative's four criteria for stylised edits (ask, do not grade on your own): every shot a different world · typography
   as protagonist · the effect transforms the character · graphic, saturated style. In realism: does the person have a role and
   does the edit glorify it?
5. The sound: the app keeps the song unless «Audio de H3» was chosen; you cannot hear it — ask the creative.

## What to change first (one thing at a time)

1. **Another seed** — if the plan is sound and it failed once (poner_semilla(), or generar(n=2–3) with the creative's OK to
   compare draws). The same recipe has come out right and wrong depending only on the seed; a new resolution is also a new draw.
2. **The prompt of the shot that failed** — make its action clearer, align framing and camera, describe the place concretely,
   remove negations (guia://prompts).
3. **The references** — fewer, cleaner (no text, no logos), the right role, a better card; an effect that moves needs a clip.
4. **The durations** — merge shots that are too short, give a slow movement more time, put a cut on the drop.
5. Only then resolution or length (they change the draw and the time).

For a local defect in an otherwise good video (a logo, a hand, an object), propose **Retocar** instead of regenerating.

## How to report

- Facts first, with times: «Shot 2 (0.50–0.92 s): the silhouette effect appears; the word TETO is not visible.»
- Then one proposal and its reason from this guide: «I would try another seed first: the plan follows the recipe and seeds
  weigh a lot.»
- Never say a result is good or bad for the creative; say what matches the plan and what does not.
