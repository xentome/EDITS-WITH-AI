# What we measured about MiniMax H3

> From our own renders, 19–31 Oct 2026, on an RTX 3060 12 GB and RunPod 24 GB GPUs. ✅ = controlled test · 🟢 = seen
> several times. These are facts and their likely cause; how to use them is your call. If the app disagrees, the app wins.

## Time
- The code decides WHEN, H3 decides WHAT. With cut times in the prompt, H3 cuts within ~0.1–0.45 s of them ✅; without
  them, cuts land at random.
- Around 5 s (124 f) H3 follows a plan. At 8–12 s with many shots it compresses the timeline and drops shots ✅. For longer
  stories, two clips joined on a cut look seamless ✅.
- About one shot per second is the ceiling (20 asked in 9 s → ~10 made ✅). Shots under 0.5 s tend to vanish 🟢.
- Two consecutive shots that ask for the same thing merge into one 🟢.
- H3 does not dance to the beat, but it cuts on the song's strongest hit anyway 🟢.

## Text weighs more than images
- The written description beats the reference image ✅. A reference only shows up when the text asks for what it shows.
- A concrete place («a dim, near-black scene with gray panels linked by thin red and blue lines») changes the background;
  a vague one («a program interface») or only a background reference does not ✅.
- Words are read literally: ambiguous phrases become objects («wet red strands» → red liquid ✅), typos and wrong pronouns
  are kept 🟢, negations («no zoom») can cause the thing they forbid 🟢.
- «Photorealistic» in a style line pulls illustrated edits toward real backgrounds.

## Bodies and camera
- Held poses with a moving camera work; invented athletic actions (jumps, spins, fast hands) deform bodies ✅.
- Framing beats camera: if they disagree, the framing wins ✅.
- A short still camera after a moving one lets the previous movement leak in ✅; a still camera needs to be described fully.
- Close shots keep identity; far shots lose the character 🟢.
- Open jackets without a named undergarment came out as bare chests (pod, 30 Oct) ✅.

## References
- At most 9 pictures and 3 videos per job.
- H3 copies concrete things it sees: logos, words, recognisable faces ✅. Blurred text is still read.
- A character photo's background bleeds in: white backgrounds give studio-like shots; a scenic background shows mainly in
  the first frame 🟢. A clean product photo comes out faithful; a crop from another picture does not 🟢.
- A pack (page: «grupo») is several photos in one RefMod, received as <Video N>. Good for identity (body + face crop) ✅. It is
  not motion: an effect that moves needs a real clip 🟢.
- A whole reel as one compressed clip reference transfers its style while following your shots ✅.
- Lowering a reference's strength blurs it rather than weakening it.
- Effects that redraw the image (silhouette, ink, halftone, line art) work 🟢; editing effects (glitch, zoom, flashes) barely
  appear. Describing an effect's cause («a flat fill that switches colour on every beat») works better than its look 🟢.

## Resolution, memory, seed
- 1 MP keeps identity and detail better than 0.5 MP 🟢. 1.5 MP (1632×928) fit on 24 GB for ~6 s ✅. Long words (ICON, LUXE)
  still garble at 1 MP.
- 12 GB: 22 f at 0.52 MP ≈ 3 min · 5 s at 0.52 MP ≈ 9 min · 5 s at 1 MP ≈ 13–18 min. 8 s at 1 MP does not fit.
  24 GB: 12 s at 1 MP fits, 12 s at 1.2 MP does not.
- The seed weighs a lot ✅: the same recipe worked with one seed and failed with another. A new resolution is a new draw.

## Sound
- Without a song, H3 invents the sound from the «Sonido» line.
- Asking for whooshes or hits over a song added bangs into it ✅; keeping the song as the video's audio is the safe choice.
